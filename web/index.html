project notes

What works, what doesn’t

This app plays recorded outputs from a small PyTorch experiment. It does not run live inference, accept uploads, identify tissue planes, or recover anatomy through opaque fluid.

The sparse models use 20 of 100 synthetic training masks. Five models were trained with equal segmentation-update budgets; motion methods used extra training compute. This viewer shows held-out sequence 06 from seed 7. Results from two seeds are included in the downloadable project.

Inspired by Zhao et al., MICCAI 2020. Small networks, losses and training differ from the paper: this is an adaptation, not a reproduction.

The lighting and liquid effects are 2D image corruptions, not physical simulations. Scores retain the clean full mask, including hidden pixels. Gate acceptance is not evidence of correctness; bright glare can be confidently mislabeled as an instrument.
